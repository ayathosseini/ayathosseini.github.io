Biography

Seyed Ayat Hosseini is a faculty member in the Department of East Asian Languages and Literatures at the University of Tehran. He received his Ph.D. in Language and Information Sciences from the University of Tokyo in 2014. His academic work brings together Japanese language and linguistics, Japanese literature, language education, and translation, with particular attention to connections between Japanese and Persian.

Alongside his university teaching and research, he serves as Vice President of the Dehkhoda Lexicon Institute & International Center for Persian Studies, University of Tehran. He has also worked in academic administration, official Japanese–Persian translation, and educational and cultural cooperation between Iran and Japan.
